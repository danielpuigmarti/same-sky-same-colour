# H5: the five target cards

Open `h5.png`. Rows 1 to 3: the three moving targets, on the left as WISE saw them in 2010, in the middle as
SPHEREx saw them in its latest visit, both on one sky grid. Rows 4 and 5: RW Aurigae and R Draconis, the first
pair of photos of their showcase.

**Numbers to read**

| Target | Reference | Measured shift | Catalogue shift | Difference | Tolerance | In SPHEREx alone |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Epsilon Indi B | Gaia DR3 6412596012146801152 | 74.3 arcsec | 73.9 arcsec | 0.5 arcsec (0.09 px) | 4.2 arcsec | 0.14 px (tolerance 0.5) |
| Barnard's Star | Gaia DR3 4472832130942575872 | 163.7 arcsec | 163.8 arcsec | 0.1 arcsec (0.01 px) | 12.2 arcsec | 0.04 px (tolerance 0.5) |
| WISE 0855-0714 | Kirkpatrick et al. 2021 | 128.1 arcsec | 128.6 arcsec | 0.7 arcsec (0.11 px) | 4.8 arcsec | 0.07 px (tolerance 0.5) |

- Each jump runs from the 2010 image (a mean of photos from April 2010 to October 2010 for Epsilon Indi B; the dates of each are on its card) to April 2026 (Epsilon Indi B), March 2026 (Barnard's Star), April 2026 (WISE 0855-0714).
- Beside WISE 0855-0714 lies the star Gaia DR3 5756956644387831296 (G 16.3), 4.3 arcsec from it at the date of the SPHEREx visit; it does not move and is fitted together with the target. In the 2010 image the target was 131 arcsec away, and what that image shows at the star's place lies 0.23 arcsec (0.04 of a SPHEREx pixel) from the star's Gaia position at that date (rule of the review: within 1 pixel). It is marked with a green square.
- Barnard's Star saturates WISE: the core of its 2010 image is spoiled, and a Gaussian fitted there lands 6.4 arcsec from the catalogue, on the brighter side of the core. That value is not used. Its 2010 centre is the centroid of the wings of its image (2 to 4 beam widths out, started from the fit and repeated until it settles), 0.3 arcsec from the catalogue. The WISE beam in its tolerance (6.4 arcsec) was set when only the first value was known; without it the tolerance would be 5.8 arcsec and the difference of 0.1 arcsec would be within it all the same.
- R Draconis: saturated in 412 of 412 photos checked.
- Showcases: Epsilon Indi B detector 6, 7 pairs; Barnard's Star detector 6, 8 pairs; WISE 0855-0714 detector 6, 8 pairs; RW Aurigae detector 1, 8 pairs; R Draconis detector 2, 8 pairs.

**It passes if**

1. Every reference is Gaia DR3 or the named published astrometry (WISE 0855: Kirkpatrick et al. 2021 as served
   by SIMBAD; `names.json` marks it unverified until the paper has been read).
2. The tolerances are those of `site/data/targets/tolerances.json`, written before the jobs measured; its first
   lines say what was known when the term for Barnard's Star was set, and the reviewer accepts that term.
3. Each jump is labelled with its two epochs.
4. The star beside WISE 0855 lies within 1 pixel of a catalogue source (the 2010 image, above) and is not taken
   for the brown dwarf.
5. No brightness appears for Barnard's Star, R Draconis or WISE 0855.

Status written by the job: K5 UNVERIFIED, K6-rdra UNVERIFIED. Reviewer's role and date are set with
`python -m sx.run targets --reviewed "<role>" <YYYY-MM-DD>` after the review.

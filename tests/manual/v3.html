Panels test page

The frame map, the spectrum and "Is it real?" on pictures made by hand (tests/fixtures/v3/make.mjs), with no stage and no network. Serve the whole project: node tools/serve.mjs --root ., then open /tests/manual/v3.html. Choose the picture with ?scenario=clean (a blip in one photo), ghost, persist, star, real (a change in every later photo) or fast (Barnard's Star moving); &extra=200 adds photos that are indexed and not loaded, &dots=21000 adds a deep field's worth of them. Click the picture to click a place.

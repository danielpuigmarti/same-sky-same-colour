## Tools and services

- NASA/IPAC Infrared Science Archive, public bucket nasa-irsa-spherex | https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/ | Public, no usage restrictions (IRSA terms of use) | the job index lists the photos of a week in the bucket (https://nasa-irsa-spherex.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/) and reads the header of each with one byte range, to build the index of photos; the headers of 46 photos were saved in pipeline/tests/fixtures/index
- Amazon S3 listing format (ListObjectsV2 answers of the bucket) | http://s3.amazonaws.com/doc/2006-03-01/ | An XML namespace name that the bucket's listing carries; nothing is fetched from it | the job index reads the bucket's listing with it

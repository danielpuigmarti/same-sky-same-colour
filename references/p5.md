## Datasets

- SPHEREx Data Release 1, Level 2 spectral images | https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/Missions/spherex.html | Public, no usage restrictions (IRSA terms of use, https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/data_use_terms.html); acknowledgement asked for | pipeline/jobs/threei.py: comet 3I/ATLAS measured in 123 photos of August and December 2025 and spring 2026 (the keys are in pipeline/frozen/obs_ids.json under "threei"); pipeline/jobs/spectra.py: Epsilon Indi B and three stars measured in the photos that cover them (frozen list "spectra"), and the point-spread-function model (EPSF extension) of each photo
- IRSA table of the SPHEREx observations of comet 3I/ATLAS (obscore_3I_Atlas.vot) | https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/data/SPHEREx/docs/comet3iatlas/obscore_3I_Atlas.vot | Public, no usage restrictions (IRSA terms of use) | pipeline/jobs/threei.py: the comet's predicted position in each photo of August and December 2025, where the aperture is centred for the row K4
- 2MASS Point Source Catalog, J, H and Ks magnitudes, as served by the Gaia archive (gaiadr1.tmass_original_valid) | https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/Missions/2mass.html | Public (2MASS data are free to use; acknowledgement asked for) | pipeline/jobs/spectra.py: the catalogue brightness that the three comparison stars' measured fluxes are compared with, as a check of the shape
- Gaia Data Release 3, gaia_source (astrometry of Barnard's Star, Epsilon Indi B, RW Aurigae, R Draconis and seven other fast stars) | https://gea.esac.esa.int/archive/ | CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO (ESA Gaia data licence; acknowledgement asked for) | pipeline/jobs/spectra.py: the stars round Epsilon Indi B (through the MAST cone search), their 2MASS counterparts (2MASS best-neighbour table of the archive) and Gaia's variability flag and RUWE

## Tools and services

- JPL Horizons | https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/horizons/ | Public service of NASA/JPL | pipeline/sx/horizons.py: positions of solar-system bodies as seen from SPHEREx (observer -163182)
- SIMBAD astronomical database | https://simbad.cds.unistra.fr/ | Free to use, acknowledgement asked for (CDS) | pipeline/jobs/spectra.py: the 2MASS J, H, K magnitudes of Epsilon Indi B
- IRSA SPHEREx Spectrophotometry Tool, help page | https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/onlinehelp/spherex/spherex/sp.html | Public, no usage restrictions (IRSA terms of use) | review/h11.md: the tool the spectra are to be compared with by hand (it is not run by any script here)

## Text sources

- Lisse et al., SPHEREx Pre-Perihelion Mapping of H2O, CO2 and CO in Interstellar Object 3I/ATLAS, arXiv:2512.07318 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.07318 | CC BY 4.0 | the August production rate of carbon dioxide, 1.6e27 molecules a second +- 10%, in the published ratio of the row K4
- Lisse et al., SPHEREx Re-Observation of Interstellar Object 3I/ATLAS in December 2025, arXiv:2601.06759 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06759 | CC BY 4.0 | the December production rate of carbon dioxide, 3.0e27 molecules a second +- 15%, and the 2-pixel aperture with an annulus, in the row K4
- Cohen, Wheaton and Megeath 2003, Spectral irradiance calibration in the infrared, AJ 126, 1090 | https://doi.org/10.1086/376474 | Published constants (the zero-point flux densities 1594, 1024 and 666.7 Jy of the 2MASS J, H and Ks bands are quoted, with citation; checked on 15 November 2026 against section VI.4a of the 2MASS All-Sky Data Release Explanatory Supplement, https://www.ipac.caltech.edu/2mass/releases/allsky/doc/sec6_4a.html, which gives the same values from this paper) | pipeline/jobs/spectra.py: turning the 2MASS magnitudes into flux densities

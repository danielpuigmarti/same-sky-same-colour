## Datasets

- SPHEREx Data Release 1, Level 2 spectral images | https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/Missions/spherex.html | Public, no usage restrictions (IRSA terms of use, https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/data_use_terms.html); acknowledgement asked for | pipeline/jobs/diffcheck.py, limits.py and falsepos.py: the photos (stamps, flags and wavelengths) at the 20 empty positions, at the comparison stars of RW Aurigae and in the fields of the false positives; the keys of the false-positive photos are in pipeline/frozen/obs_ids.json under "falsepos"
- Gaia Data Release 3 | https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/gaia/dr3 | CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO (ESA/Gaia/DPAC); the 73 stars of pipeline/tests/p1_data/gaia_barnard_d2.json are an excerpt, kept under the same licence | pipeline/jobs/diffcheck.py: the check that a position has no star brighter than G = 13 within 5 arcmin, and the positions and magnitudes of the comparison stars; pipeline/jobs/falsepos.py: the stars near each false positive and the fast stars of the ring question

## Libraries

- Astropy | https://www.astropy.org/ | BSD-3-Clause | sky transforms (all_world2pix), galactic and ecliptic coordinates, space motion of the Gaia stars
- NumPy | https://numpy.org/ | BSD-3-Clause | arrays of the jobs and their tests
- SciPy | https://scipy.org/ | BSD-3-Clause | labelling and filtering flagged regions in pipeline/jobs/falsepos.py
- Matplotlib | https://matplotlib.org/ | Matplotlib licence (based on the Python Software Foundation licence) | the pictures of the checks k8-pb6.png, k8-flipbooks.png, k10-limits.png and the review sheets h8.png and h9.png

## Tools and services

- MAST Gaia DR3 cone search | https://mast.stsci.edu/api/v0/ | Public service of the Space Telescope Science Institute | pipeline/sx/gaia.py, called by diffcheck and falsepos: the Gaia stars round a position

## Datasets

- SPHEREx Data Release 1, Level 2 spectral images | https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/Missions/spherex.html | Public, no usage restrictions (IRSA terms of use, https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/data_use_terms.html); acknowledgement asked for | pipeline/jobs/targets.py, motion.py and rwaur.py: the frames that hold Epsilon Indi B, Barnard's Star, WISE 0855-0714, RW Aurigae and R Draconis (the keys are in pipeline/frozen/obs_ids.json under "targets", "motion" and "rwaur"); the stamps in site/data/targets/*/ and site/data/lie/ (stamps.bin, flags.bin, pair.png, spherex.png, jump.png) are cuts of these frames
- AllWISE Atlas (L3a) Coadd Images, band W2 | https://doi.org/10.26131/IRSA153 | Public, no usage restrictions (IRSA terms of use, https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/data_use_terms.html); acknowledgement asked for | pipeline/jobs/targets.py: the image of 2010 of the three moving targets (tiles 3306m561_ac51 and 3323m576_ac51, 2688p045_ac51, 1342m076_ac51), in which their positions of 2010 are measured for the row K5; site/data/targets/eps-indi-b, barnard and wise0855: wise2010.png and the left half of jump.png are cuts of these images, drawn on a sky grid
- Gaia Data Release 3, table gaia_source | https://doi.org/10.5270/esa-qa4lep3 | CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO (ESA/Gaia/DPAC); site/data/gaia/fast.json and pipeline/jobs/_rwaur_stars.py are excerpts, kept under the same licence | site/data/gaia/fast.json: every source that moves more than 0.5 arcsec a year, with position, proper motion, parallax and G magnitude; pipeline/jobs/_rwaur_stars.py: the twenty comparison stars of RW Aurigae; pipeline/jobs/motion.py and targets.py: the field stars that serve as the control of the motion check and the star beside WISE 0855-0714

## Libraries

- Astropy | https://www.astropy.org/ | BSD-3-Clause | pipeline/jobs/_targets_lib.py: moving a catalogue star to a date (space motion, position of the Earth), sky grids and reading the AllWISE images; pipeline/jobs/gaia_fast.py: reading the tables the two archives answer with
- NumPy | https://numpy.org/ | BSD-3-Clause | arrays in the jobs targets, motion, rwaur and gaia_fast
- SciPy | https://scipy.org/ | BSD-3-Clause | pipeline/jobs/_targets_lib.py: the least-squares fit of a Gaussian to a star
- Matplotlib | https://matplotlib.org/ | Matplotlib licence (based on the Python Software Foundation licence) | the pictures of the rows K5, K5-motion, K6, K6-rdra and K7 (site/data/checks/img/k5-*.png, k6-*.png, k7-*.png) and of the sheets review/h5.png, h6.png and h7.png
- Requests | https://requests.readthedocs.io/ | Apache-2.0 | pipeline/jobs/targets.py and gaia_fast.py: asking IRSA for the AllWISE images, and MAST and the GAVO Data Center for the fast stars

## Tools and services

- IRSA image search (SIA), collection spherex_dr1 | https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/SIA | Public, no usage restrictions (IRSA terms of use) | pipeline/jobs/targets.py, motion.py and rwaur.py, through pipeline/sx/irsa.py: the first answer to which frames hold each target; the lists in use were answers of this service on 14 November 2026 and are frozen
- IRSA image search (SIA), collection wise_allwise | https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/SIA?COLLECTION=wise_allwise | Public, no usage restrictions (IRSA terms of use) | pipeline/jobs/targets.py: which AllWISE Atlas image holds each moving target and the dates of its first and last photo; the square cuts of those images round the targets are asked of the addresses this search returns, with the cutout parameters of IRSA's image server (center, size)
- MAST Gaia DR3 cone search | https://mast.stsci.edu/api/v0/ | Public service of the Space Telescope Science Institute | pipeline/jobs/motion.py, targets.py and _rwaur_stars.py, through pipeline/sx/gaia.py: the Gaia DR3 stars round Epsilon Indi B and WISE 0855-0714, and the catalogue rows of the comparison stars of RW Aurigae
- MAST table access (TAP) to Gaia DR3 | https://mast.stsci.edu/vo-tap/api/v0.1/gaiadr3/ | Public service of the Space Telescope Science Institute; the Gaia data it serves keep the Gaia licence | pipeline/jobs/gaia_fast.py: the search of Gaia DR3 (table gaia_source) for the stars that move more than 0.5 arcsec a year
- GAVO Data Center table access (TAP), table gaia.dr3lite | https://dc.g-vo.org/tap | Public service of the German Astrophysical Virtual Observatory; the Gaia data it serves keep the Gaia licence | pipeline/jobs/gaia_fast.py: a second copy of Gaia DR3, asked the same question; the list of fast stars is written only if both copies give the same sources
- SIMBAD astronomical database | https://simbad.cds.unistra.fr/ | Free to use, acknowledgement asked for (CDS) | site/data/targets/tolerances.json: the 2MASS Ks magnitude of Barnard's Star (4.52), read through the CfA mirror, in the reason why the star saturates WISE

- Wright et al. 2010, The Wide-field Infrared Survey Explorer (WISE): Mission Description and Initial On-orbit Performance, AJ 140, 1868 | https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-6256/140/6/1868 | Published value quoted with citation (checked on 15 November 2026 against the paper's abstract in NASA ADS, 2010AJ....140.1868W: angular resolution 6.1, 6.4, 6.5 and 12.0 arcsec in the four bands) | site/data/targets/tolerances.json: the beam width of WISE in the W2 band, 6.4 arcsec, added to the tolerance of a star that saturates WISE
- Kirkpatrick et al. 2021, The Field Substellar Mass Function Based on the Full-sky 20 pc Census of 525 L, T, and Y Dwarfs, ApJS 253, 7 | https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4365/abd107 | Published values quoted with citation, as served by SIMBAD (the paper itself was not opened) | the rows K5 and K5-motion: the position, proper motion and parallax of WISE 0855-0714 that its measured shifts are compared with (the values are in site/data/names.json)

## Datasets

- SPHEREx Data Release 1, Level 2 spectral images | https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/Missions/spherex.html | Public, no usage restrictions (IRSA terms of use, https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/data_use_terms.html); acknowledgement asked for | pipeline/jobs/lemmon.py: comet C/2025 A6 (Lemmon) measured in the 16 frames of detector 5 that hold it on 25 and 26 February 2026, and its places in 5 frames of August 2025, chosen among 63 frames (the keys are in pipeline/frozen/obs_ids.json under "lemmon"); site/data/lemmon/hero, strip and templates hold 64 by 64 pixel stamps of 21 of these frames, and the pictures in site/data/lemmon/, site/data/checks/img/k2-contrast.png and k3-templates.png, review/h1.png and review/h2.png are drawn from them
- Gaia Data Release 3 | https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/gaia/dr3 | CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO (ESA/Gaia/DPAC); the 73 stars of pipeline/tests/p1_data/gaia_barnard_d2.json are an excerpt, kept under the same licence | pipeline/jobs/lemmon.py: the catalogue stars brighter than G 15 within 4 pixels of the comet in each frame, moved to the date of the frame; the source numbers and G magnitudes of these stars in site/data/lemmon/spectrum.json, and of one of them in site/data/numbers/p2.json (lemmon.star.magnitude), are an excerpt kept under the same licence

## Libraries

- Matplotlib | https://matplotlib.org/ | Matplotlib licence (based on the Python Software Foundation licence) | pipeline/jobs/lemmon_draw.py: the pictures of the comet (site/data/lemmon/*.png and */*.png, k2-contrast.png, k3-templates.png, review/h1.png, review/h2.png)

## Tools and services

- IRSA image search (SIA), collection spherex_dr1 | https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/SIA | Public, no usage restrictions (IRSA terms of use) | pipeline/jobs/lemmon.py: the frames of detector 5 along the comet's path on 25 and 26 February 2026 and at the places where it was photographed inside the carbon-dioxide band, frozen once answered
- MAST Gaia DR3 cone search | https://mast.stsci.edu/api/v0/ | Public service of the Space Telescope Science Institute | pipeline/jobs/lemmon.py, through pipeline/sx/gaia.py: the Gaia DR3 stars round the comet's position in each frame
- JPL Horizons | https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/horizons/ | Public service of NASA/JPL | pipeline/jobs/lemmon.py, through pipeline/sx/horizons.py: the position of comet C/2025 A6 (Lemmon) as seen from SPHEREx at the middle of each exposure, and its path over 25 and 26 February 2026 in site/data/lemmon/track.json

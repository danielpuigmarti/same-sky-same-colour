## Datasets

- SPHEREx Data Release 1, Level 2 spectral images | https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/Missions/spherex.html | Public, no usage restrictions (IRSA terms of use, https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/data_use_terms.html); acknowledgement asked for | pipeline/jobs/lines.py: NGC 6543 measured in 131 frames of detectors 2 and 5, and pipeline/jobs/placement.py: one frame of each detector in the field of Barnard's Star, stamps compared with Gaia positions (the keys are in pipeline/frozen/obs_ids.json under "lines" and "placement")
- SPHEREx Data Release 1, Spectral WCS product cal-swcs-v5-2026-191 (CWAVE layer) | https://nasa-irsa-spherex.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/dr1/spectral_wcs/cal-swcs-v5-2026-191/ | Public, no usage restrictions (IRSA terms of use) | pipeline/jobs/lines.py: IRSA's own wavelength map, which the wavelength tables are compared with for detectors 1, 3, 4 and 6
- Gaia Data Release 3 | https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/gaia/dr3 | CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO (ESA/Gaia/DPAC); the 73 stars of pipeline/tests/p1_data/gaia_barnard_d2.json are an excerpt, kept under the same licence | pipeline/sx/gaia.py and pipeline/jobs/placement.py: catalogue positions and proper motions of the stars that the placement of stamps is checked against
- NIST Atomic Spectra Database, hydrogen I, vacuum wavelengths | https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/ASD/lines_form.html | Public (NIST Standard Reference Data, free to use with citation) | pipeline/jobs/lines.py: the laboratory wavelengths of Paschen-beta (1.28216 um) and Brackett-alpha (4.05226 um)

## Tools and services

- IRSA image search (SIA), collection spherex_dr1 | https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/SIA | Public, no usage restrictions (IRSA terms of use) | pipeline/sx/irsa.py: the list of frames that cover NGC 6543 and Barnard's Star, frozen after the first answer
- MAST Gaia DR3 cone search | https://mast.stsci.edu/api/v0/ | Public service of the Space Telescope Science Institute | pipeline/sx/gaia.py: the way the Gaia DR3 stars round a position are fetched
- JPL Horizons | https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/horizons/ | Public service of NASA/JPL | pipeline/sx/horizons.py: positions of solar-system bodies as seen from SPHEREx (observer -163182)

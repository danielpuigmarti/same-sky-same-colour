## Datasets

- SIMBAD astronomical database, tables basic, ident and flux (names, kinds, positions, proper motions, parallaxes, radial velocities and V-band magnitudes of about 1400 well-known places of the sky; Wenger et al. 2000, A&AS 143, 9) | https://simbad.cds.unistra.fr/simbad/ | Open Database License (ODbL); the acknowledgement "This research has made use of the SIMBAD database, operated at CDS, Strasbourg, France" is carried in the file | site/data/places.json, written by pipeline/jobs/places.py, read by the suggestions under the search box

## Tools and services

- SIMBAD TAP service (CfA mirror) | https://simbad.cfa.harvard.edu/simbad/sim-tap | Free for research use, with acknowledgement | read by pipeline/jobs/places.py
- SIMBAD TAP service (Strasbourg) | https://simbad.u-strasbg.fr/simbad/sim-tap | Free for research use, with acknowledgement | read by pipeline/jobs/places.py when the mirror does not answer (the job also tries simbad.cds.unistra.fr)

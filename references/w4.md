## Datasets

- SIMBAD astronomical database, as answered by the Sesame name resolver (position, proper motion, parallax and radial velocity of a named object) | https://simbad.cfa.harvard.edu/simbad/ | Free without registration under the ODbL licence; citing Wenger et al. 2000, A&AS 143, 9 is asked for | tools/demo.mjs: the position of the Ring Nebula (M 57), 18 53 35.1 +33 01 45, which the tool types in the box for the view of a typed position in the demo video; the pictures of that view are read from the SPHEREx archive while it is filmed

## Libraries

- Playwright | https://playwright.dev/ | Apache-2.0 | tools/demo.mjs: starts the browser that films the demo video, moves its mouse and its keys, and draws the contact sheet review/video/sheet.png; used in development only

## Tools and services

- Chromium, as Chrome for Testing | https://www.chromium.org/ | BSD-3-Clause | the browser in which tools/demo.mjs films the site for the demo video
- FFmpeg, the build that Playwright installs (version n7.0.1, build 1013) | https://ffmpeg.org/ | LGPL-2.1-or-later | tools/demo.mjs: puts the pictures of the pages together into the demo video (VP9 in WebM, no sound), and reads the finished video back: its length, its size, and the frames of review/video/sheet.png; used in development only
- libvpx, the VP9 encoder and decoder inside that FFmpeg (version 1.14.1) | https://www.webmproject.org/code/ | BSD-3-Clause | the video stream of the demo video is written and read back with it; used in development only
- FFmpeg 7.1, the essentials build of gyan.dev that imageio-ffmpeg ships | https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/ | GPL-3.0-or-later (this build) | the final demo video: the team's three voice notes trimmed, cleaned, levelled to -14 LUFS and laid under the pictures, and the pictures put together as H.264 in MP4; used in development only
- x264, the H.264 encoder inside that FFmpeg | https://www.videolan.org/developers/x264.html | GPL-2.0-or-later | the video stream of the final demo video; used in development only
- imageio-ffmpeg | https://github.com/imageio/imageio-ffmpeg | BSD-2-Clause | where that FFmpeg comes from; used in development only
- faster-whisper | https://github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper | MIT | the words of the team's voice notes and when each one is said, for the subtitles of the final demo video (read by a person and corrected where it misheard); used in development only
- Whisper medium.en (OpenAI), in the CTranslate2 form of faster-whisper | https://huggingface.co/Systran/faster-whisper-medium.en | MIT | the speech model of that transcription; used in development only

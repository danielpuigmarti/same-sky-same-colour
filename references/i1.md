## Datasets

- SIMBAD astronomical database, as answered by the Sesame name resolver (position, proper motion, parallax and radial velocity of a named object) | https://simbad.cfa.harvard.edu/simbad/ | Free without registration under the ODbL licence; citing Wenger et al. 2000, A&AS 143, 9 is asked for | the position of a name that is not in the site's own list, asked for at the moment a visitor types it and not stored; the recorded answers for Vega, Proxima Centauri and M 31 in tests/unit/i1-sesame.test.mjs and for Vega in tests/e2e/i1-live.spec.mjs
- NASA/IPAC Extragalactic Database (NED), as answered by the Sesame name resolver | https://ned.ipac.caltech.edu/ | Public, funded by NASA and operated by Caltech; no formal terms of use, acknowledgement asked for | the position of a name that SIMBAD does not know, asked for at the moment a visitor types it and not stored; one recorded line for M 31 in tests/unit/i1-sesame.test.mjs

## Tools and services

- Sesame name resolver of the CDS (Centre de Données astronomiques de Strasbourg), mirror at the Center for Astrophysics | https://vizier.cfa.harvard.edu/viz-bin/Sesame | Free service, no registration; its answers come from SIMBAD and NED under their own terms | site/js/lib/sesame.js: asked from the visitor's browser for a name that is not in the site's own list; only the name is sent
- Sesame name resolver of the CDS, Strasbourg | https://cds.unistra.fr/cgi-bin/Sesame | Free service, no registration; its answers come from SIMBAD and NED under their own terms | site/js/lib/sesame.js: asked when the mirror does not answer; a redirect to any other address is refused
- Google Chrome | https://www.google.com/chrome/ | Free to use (Google Chrome and ChromeOS Additional Terms of Service) | the browser in which tools/timing.mjs opens the viewer to time it; used in development only, and the browser that comes with Playwright can be used instead

## Datasets

- SPHEREx Data Release 1, Level 2 spectral images | https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/Missions/spherex.html | Public, no usage restrictions (IRSA terms of use, https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/data_use_terms.html); acknowledgement asked for | pipeline/jobs/gallery.py: the photos of the stars that moved and of the empty fields (frozen list "gallery"); pipeline/jobs/pluto.py: the photos that hold Pluto and their same-colour twins (frozen list "pluto")
- Gaia Data Release 3 | https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/gaia/dr3 | CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO (ESA/Gaia/DPAC); the 73 stars of pipeline/tests/p1_data/gaia_barnard_d2.json are an excerpt, kept under the same licence | pipeline/jobs/gallery.py: the list of fast stars, their positions at each date, the neighbours that decide isolation and the star density and proper motions of the empty fields
- AllWISE Atlas images, band W2 (4.6 µm), taken in 2010 | https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/ibe/docs/wise/allwise/p3am_cdd/ | Public, no usage restrictions (IRSA terms of use); doi:10.26131/IRSA153 | pipeline/jobs/gallery.py: the 2010 picture of each star that moved and of each empty field in the "Can you spot it?" set

## Tools and services

- ESA Gaia archive TAP service | https://gea.esac.esa.int/tap-server/tap | ESA Gaia data licence | pipeline/jobs/_gallery_lib.py: one query for each band of declination for the stars with a proper motion over 1 arcsec a year
- MAST Gaia DR3 cone search | https://mast.stsci.edu/api/v0/ | Public service of the Space Telescope Science Institute | pipeline/sx/gaia.py, called by pipeline/jobs/gallery.py: the Gaia sources round a star or an empty field
- IRSA image search (SIA) and cutout service for AllWISE | https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/ibe/cutouts.html | Public service of NASA/IPAC | pipeline/jobs/_gallery_lib.py: which AllWISE image holds a position and the cut-out of it, fetched offline by the job (never by the site)
- JPL Horizons | https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/horizons/ | Public service of NASA/JPL | pipeline/jobs/pluto.py, through pipeline/sx/horizons.py: Pluto's place as SPHEREx saw it (target 999, observer -163182) at each photo's time and once a day for finding the photos

## Libraries

- Astropy | https://www.astropy.org/ | BSD-3-Clause | pipeline/jobs/_gallery_lib.py: moving Gaia stars to each date with proper motion and parallax, the sky transforms of photos and of the pictures' grid
- NumPy | https://numpy.org/ | BSD-3-Clause | arrays in pipeline/jobs/gallery.py and pipeline/jobs/pluto.py
- SciPy | https://scipy.org/ | BSD-3-Clause | the Gaussian fits and the smoothing of the Pluto difference frames
- Matplotlib | https://matplotlib.org/ | Matplotlib licence (based on the Python Software Foundation licence) | the contact sheets in review/ and the pictures of the Checks rows k-gallery-offsets.png and k-pluto-found.png
- Requests | https://requests.readthedocs.io/ | Apache-2.0 | the calls to the Gaia, IRSA and Horizons services from the pipeline
